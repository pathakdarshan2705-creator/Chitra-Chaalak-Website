> "The night has a heartbeat, powered by electricity."

This series aims to capture the isolating yet deeply comforting feeling of wandering through an unfamiliar city at 3 AM. The glow of vending machines and neon signs served as the only light sources.

## Technique
Shot with a 35mm f/1.4 lens to allow for handheld shooting at low shutter speeds, giving a slight, organic motion blur to the rushing taxis and pedestrians.
