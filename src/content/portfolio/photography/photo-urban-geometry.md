> "Photography is the art of finding order in chaos."

Urban Geometry is an ongoing photo series that isolates architectural details, abstracting them into pure geometric compositions. By waiting for the exact right moment when the sun casts sharp, diagonal shadows across the facades of buildings, these spaces are transformed from everyday structures into minimalist canvases.

## Technical Notes

All images in this series were shot on a medium format digital sensor to preserve the microscopic textures of the concrete and steel.

- Camera: Hasselblad X1D II 50C
- Lens: XCD 45mm f/4 P
- Processing: Capture One Pro
