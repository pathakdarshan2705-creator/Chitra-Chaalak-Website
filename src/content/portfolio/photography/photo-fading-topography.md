> "From above, the earth becomes an abstract painting."

A collection of top-down drone shots taken over salt flats, deltas, and deserts. The focus is entirely on texture and the natural patterns created by erosion and time.

These images challenge our sense of scale. Without a horizon line, a massive river delta looks indistinguishable from the microscopic veins of a leaf.
