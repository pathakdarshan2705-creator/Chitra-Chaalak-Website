> "Every error is a feature waiting to be discovered."

By intentionally corrupting the raw hexadecimal code of image files and feeding video files through audio editing software, this series embraces the beauty of the broken.

## The Artifacts
The resulting images are unpredictable and irreproducible. Vivid bands of magenta and cyan slice through distorted pixels, creating a frantic, cyberpunk energy that questions our reliance on perfect digital reproduction.
