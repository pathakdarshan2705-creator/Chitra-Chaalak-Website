> "Nature is the best algorithm."

Synthetic Nature is a collection of 100 generative artworks created using WebGL and custom shaders. By manipulating noise algorithms and mathematical equations, these pieces perfectly mimic the growth patterns of fungi, coral, and mycelium.

## The Medium
Code. Specifically, GLSL fragment shaders running in real-time in the browser. The still images presented here are just snapshots of living, breathing, endlessly shifting digital organisms.
