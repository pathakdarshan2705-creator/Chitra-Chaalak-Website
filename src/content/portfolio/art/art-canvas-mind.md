> "The painting has a life of its own. I try to let it come through."

A departure from digital mediums, this series involves massive 6-foot canvases, heavy impasto acrylics, and the physical act of throwing and scraping paint.

## The Process
No brushes were used. Paint was applied with palette knives, cardboard scraps, and bare hands to create deeply textured, chaotic surfaces that demand to be viewed in person, where the light can catch the ridges of dried acrylic.
