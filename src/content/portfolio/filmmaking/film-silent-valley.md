> _Ahmedabad Heritage Special_

> **“A city remembers through the things it refuses to forget.”**

_Ahmedabad’s 12 Darwaja_ explores the historic gateways of Ahmedabad and the stories held within them, looking beyond the structures themselves to the history, architecture and living heritage of the old city.

## The Process

I worked on this film while working with **Jalso Production House**, where I was involved in the documentary-making process as a **Researcher, Writer, Cinematographer and Editor**.

The film looks at Ahmedabad through its historic Darwajas: structures that once formed important entrances to the walled city and today remain surrounded by the everyday movement of Ahmedabad. The research focused on understanding the history behind these gates and the stories that make them more than just architectural monuments.

What interested me most was the relationship between the old and the present-day city. These gates have existed through enormous changes in Ahmedabad, yet people, traffic, markets and everyday life continue to move around them.

## The Approach

The film was approached as a **heritage documentary**, combining historical research with visual exploration of the gates themselves.

Rather than presenting the Darwajas simply as static monuments, we wanted the film to place them within the city they still inhabit, their architecture, details, surroundings and the constant movement of life around them.

The film takes the viewer through the city's historic gateways while uncovering the stories behind them, allowing the architecture and the history to work together.

> **“The gates may have been built to mark the edge of a city, but the city kept growing around them.”**

## The Film

Ahmedabad has a history stretching back more than six centuries, and its walled city is deeply connected to the Darwajas that once controlled movement into and out of it. The film looks at these gateways as an important part of that history and explores why they continue to matter to the identity of Ahmedabad today.

The project was also an opportunity for me to work with a subject that sits close to my interests: **Indian history, culture, architecture and the stories embedded in everyday places.**

## My Role

**Research · Writing · Cinematography · Editing**

I was involved in the film across the process, from researching the subject and developing the narrative to filming the visuals and shaping the final edit.

## Working with Jalso

This film was one of several documentary projects I worked on during my time with **Jalso Production House**.

Through Jalso, I had the opportunity to work on multiple documentary films and projects for the production house as well as for **external clients through the production house**, taking on different responsibilities across research, writing, cinematography and editing.

Working across these projects gave me the chance to experience documentary filmmaking beyond an academic setting, working with real subjects, real locations, real stories and the practical constraints that come with producing films for actual audiences and clients.
