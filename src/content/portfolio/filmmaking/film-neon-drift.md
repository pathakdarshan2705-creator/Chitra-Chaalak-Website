> "Speed isn't just about moving fast. It's about how everything else stands still."

Neon Drift was an ambitious project commissioned by an electronic synth-wave artist. The brief was simple: Tokyo, fast cars, and unrelenting neon. 

## The Challenge

Shooting out of the back of a tracking vehicle at 80mph in the middle of the night presented massive stabilization and exposure challenges. We pushed the dual-native ISO of the camera to its absolute limits to capture the reflections of the city lights dancing across the metallic paint of the cars.

## Color Grading

The color pipeline was established early on. We used a custom show LUT that crushed the blacks and pushed extreme cyan and magenta into the highlights, giving the entire video a distinct retro-futuristic aesthetic.
