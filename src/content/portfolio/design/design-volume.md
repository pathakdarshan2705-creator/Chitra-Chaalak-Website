> "Print is dead. Long live print."

Volume is a bi-annual culture magazine that refuses to follow traditional editorial rules. Every spread is treated as an individual piece of poster art.

## The Grid
We started with a 9-column grid, but explicitly allowed typography and imagery to break out of the margins, overlapping each other in controlled chaos. 

## Typography
The pairing of a highly ornamental serif with a completely stripped-back grotesque sans-serif creates a beautiful tension across the spreads.
