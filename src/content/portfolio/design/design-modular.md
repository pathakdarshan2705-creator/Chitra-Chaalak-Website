> "Good design is as little design as possible."

The brief was to create a flexible identity system that felt as permanent and structured as the buildings the firm designs.

## Typography
We chose *Helvetica Now Display* for its clinical precision and tight tracking. The grid system is strictly 12-column, applied across both digital and print collateral.

## The Outcome
The result is a highly modular system where the logo itself acts as a structural framing device for the architectural photography, constantly adapting to the aspect ratio of the medium.
