# The Benchmark of Indian Cinema

There is before *Sholay*, and there is after *Sholay*. This movie redefined the "masala" genre, establishing tropes that would be used for decades to come.

Gabbar Singh is arguably the greatest villain in Indian cinema history. The dialogues are etched in the cultural memory of the nation. It's an absolute masterclass in pacing, character development, and sheer entertainment.
