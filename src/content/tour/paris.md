There is a timeless romanticism baked into the very cobblestones of Paris. My journey started near Montmartre, walking the steep streets early in the morning before the tourists arrived. 

Sitting at a small corner cafe, sipping espresso and biting into a warm croissant while watching the city wake up is an experience I will never forget. As the sun set, the Eiffel Tower ignited the sky in a warm, electric glow.
