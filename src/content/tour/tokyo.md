Tokyo is a city of intense contrasts. One minute you're standing in the bustling neon-drenched intersection of Shibuya, and the next you're tucked away in a quiet alleyway slurping the best bowl of ramen you've ever had in your life.

The rain here doesn't wash away the vibrancy; it amplifies it, turning the asphalt into a mirror of cyber-city lights. Exploring Tokyo through my lens felt like stepping fifty years into the future.
